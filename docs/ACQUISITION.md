## First-100 plan and decision logic

Start the 24–72 hour active window only when Search Console impressions are readable. Target 100 qualified search-result impressions across exact surfaces: root 40, comparison guide 30, extra-cost guide 30. Record impressions as exposure, search clicks as visits, non-demo `comparison_complete` as use, and non-demo copy/print as action. Exclude IndexNow submissions, crawler hits, tests and Founder traffic.

- **PASS:** at least 8 visits, 3 non-demo completions and 1 copy/print after 100 exposures, plus one direct WTP signal for the proposed confirmation pack.
- **ITERATE:** exposure is measurable but visits/actions miss PASS; change one message or surface and retest once.
- **STOP recommendation:** after that retest, 200 qualified exposures still produce fewer than 2 non-demo completions and no action or WTP signal.
- **NO DISTRIBUTION EVIDENCE:** denominator unreadable or fewer than 100 exposures delivered. Never call it demand failure.
